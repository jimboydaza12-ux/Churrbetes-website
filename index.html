A sweet little sideshow

Classic churros,
a new way to enjoy.

Inspired by the charm of an 18th-century European circus, Churrbetes turns the classic churro into bite-sized treats served in a cup with ice cream on top. It’s a playful dessert made for students, families, and anyone who loves discovering something sweet.

Come for the churros. Stay for the show.
